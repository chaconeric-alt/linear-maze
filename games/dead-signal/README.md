# Dead Signal

A technician trapped in a biotech lab gone wrong fights his way to the top floor. `DESIGN.md` is the plan for the whole game: the story, the six maps chapter by chapter, the bestiary, the arsenal, and the engine and pack changes it needs, in milestones.

**Built: the whole game**, from the night shift before the lockdown to the boardroom (milestones 1 to 6, and the prologue): the prologue, Floors 1 to 5, 5a, Sub-level B, and the way back up to Five.

**The prologue, The Night Shift.** The ground floor at 01:41, the tutorial. The building as it was: lights on, shutters up, the security units green and polite, and the night staff alive, every one of them talking about the board, which is meeting upstairs at two in the morning, and about who's been invited up. ARIADNE, friendly, gives Theo his night: his coveralls and bolt driver from Facilities, his trainee Jo Brennan, three bolts in Kowalski's test board, then four work orders that take him past everyone (Felix Arden and Leon Marsh in the break room, Danny Ruiz and Dana Kwan in the security office, Nadia Ferrante in first aid, Dennis Abara and Hal Ostrowski on the dock; Karin Lund, Marisa Quint, Hal Okafor and Priya Sethi on the way). Voss asks everyone over the PA to be on their best behaviour. Then the PA calls five people up to Floor 4 and Brennan to Floor 2, the core switch in Comms Closet 1-C fails, and ARIADNE sends Theo to fit the spare, shuts the door on him, and sends what she has to send. Sirens, shutters, screaming, gunfire; then Floor 1. The pause screen can skip it.

**Floor 1, Lockdown.** The ground floor of Asterion Biologics at 02:14. Theo starts sealed in Comms Closet 1-C with only his Bolt Driver; Dr. Voss talks him out over the intercom. Throw the closet breaker; get past the live cable in the west corridor; go through the lobby past two Sentinels, a Warden Drone and the cameras; reach the security office terminal; Voss opens Stair A. Off the route: Officer Ruiz's badge opens the armory cage (Riot Shotgun), his locker code is in the break room (sidearm and vest), Facilities has the hard hat, the mail room shuts you in with Nursery Grubs, HR has a Hollow Coat, and three of ARIADNE's twelve fragments are hidden on the floor. Stair B (down to the sub-level) and the elevators stay shut: that is for later in the story.

**Floor 2, Synthid Research.** Asterion's public face, and what it makes: synthids, microscopic living machines that make drinking water, eat plastic, breathe in carbon and eat cancer. A recorded voice is telling everyone to go to Shelter B, and green lights in the floor lead there. The shelter is sealed: supervisor override required. Round through the Synthid Discovery Center (DOCENT-3's tour, with Couriers), into administration (Dr. Mori's terminal opens the lab wing; her note says not to open the shelter), then the labs: the Membrane Lab is under brine (wade it, or turn both valves), the Mycology Lab breathes spores (the Hazmat Hood is in its anteroom), the Air Garden is quiet, and Oncology has the Cryo Sprayer in its cold store, and Mori, and her card. The card opens Shelter B. Fourteen people went in; none of them has a face any more, or nearly none. The Congregation sleeps among them, wearing their faces. The quarantine release on the far wall wakes it; kill it and Stair A opens up. Off the route: ARIADNE's node 02, the night schedule and dispatch (the TEMPLATE RUN), Plant Room B (the shelter breathes Floor 3's air), the tour theatre and Little Labs, fragments 4 and 5.

**Floor 3, Gestation.** Where the specimens are grown. A ring of corridor round the vat hall, and off it the restricted labs, each with its own horror, most in containment and some not: the Midwife (Lab 3-A: it lays eggs under your skin, and they hatch in ninety seconds unless a purge station or an injector flushes them), Tenants (3-B: they crawl into the dead and walk them about), Flesh Lanterns (3-D, in the dark: they are its only light), juvenile Congregations in pens (3-E: the file on them says where Colony 4 went), and Cell S-41, empty, its specimen "transferred to Five". Two Sitters, huge, slow and all but bulletproof, walk the vat hall and the ring; you hear them first. The security robots and the specimens fight each other; ARIADNE's Orderlies (green visors, a blue ring) fight both, on your side, and her relay shows their orders. Voss wants you straight across to Stair B ("be careful what you look at"), but it wants Level 3 clearance: Brandt's office, a card that's still being read in Lab 3-B every half hour, and Dr. Okafor, who is still walking. In the briefing suite it's all "macroscopic synthids". On the stairs up, Voss makes his case, and what he says depends on what you read.

**Floor 4, the Proving Grounds.** Garrick Moss's floor, where the board watched people die, and tonight the trial system has a subject: you. A recorded game-show announcer, odds boards with your name on them. Before you go, the dead freight elevator comes up the shaft on what power ARIADNE can spare, and opens on a hard case: the AEGIS field, a containment field worn over your armour. "YOU WILL NEED THIS. DEATH HERE." It takes a lot of punishment, grips slide off it, and when a hit would kill you it overloads instead and throws everything off you (then it needs a minute). Through the gate at the end of the corridor the trial starts: the gate drops behind you, three night staff in orange jumpsuits are let out ahead of you and the dogs behind (Grinners: no eyes, a grin all the way round the head, a person's teeth). The Run is a maze of steel with blades that come up out of the floor, shock plates, darts, turrets and cage rooms that seal you in with the dogs; the contestants run for the Box at the centre, and the dogs hunt whoever's nearest, and eat what they catch. In the Box (the board's armchairs, champagne, their bets on you) the console starts the final round in the arena below: three waves, the last with Smiler, the house favourite. Then the west side opens, and ARIADNE opens what the board kept sealed as you pass, while Voss begs you not to look: the Nursery (something under a child's bed), the Party Room (the Jester, three heads, a named fight) and the Long Room (six masked Diners strapped in at a dinner table, and under the cover, the main course, alive). The Dream Studios record the board's nightmares and project them as holograms that turn to look at you. Moss's log says what LABRYS was for. The Incinerator is in his armory, if you can get the code from Kwan. On Stair A, Voss explains himself.

**Floor 5, the Executive Floor.** Dark wood and red wood, marble, jade and amber; oval rooms lit from rings of lights in the ceiling. Voss tells you what he wants at last: the mainframe is called ARIADNE, its core is in the sub-level, and it can be purged from the Control Room on the board's private floor above. Do it, and you'll never want for anything again. The way up is the private stairs in the four corner offices, and the offices are locked: each secretary kept a key, and each key is somewhere different (in a woman wrapped in silk in the Typing Pool, on the dining table by a place card with your name on it, by a body in the Clerks' Row where something with a hundred hands is counting, in the Robot Chambers where the pods wake). The Concierge greets you at Reception and then comes over the desk. The Gallery's glass floor looks down on the Proving Grounds, as you left them, with masked guests at the rail. The Statue Hall's Medusa isn't bronze (don't meet her eyes: you turn to stone); in the Rotunda's fountain, around Andromeda and Cetus, Scylla's six heads and Charybdis's whirlpool. The kitchen cooks for nobody, robotically. The Boardroom is behind blast shutters, rail guns and two Praetorians that send your shots back at you: the board are safe in there, and Voss talks to you from inside.

**Floor 5a, the board's floor.** The other half of Five. The Library (the Director's shelf: which book does he read? the right one swings the bookcase in, a wrong one brings a Faceless Usher), his round bedroom behind it and his card, the Archive, the Dream Archive, Building Systems, the Nest (the old spa, where the Swaddler weaves the staff into eggs: cut them out; silk holds you fast, and burns), ARIADNE's node in the Server Room, the Atrium over the Rotunda, the Robot Bay. And the **Control Room**: ARIADNE speaks to you plainly, Voss offers ten million, and the console offers both. Purge the core and it's over (*The Dead Signal*, with *Choose again*). Restore the elevators' power, and Voss turns every robot on the floor on you, and the board's private car takes you down to ARIADNE.

**Sub-level B, Tartarus.** Where Asterion keeps what it made and couldn't unmake. Black basalt and bronze; a long hall between round vault doors, each vault with windows so the board could come down and look. Behind the glass, the Titans, which don't age, don't die and grow back from anything: the Leviathan, forty metres of jade serpent covered in eyes, going round its pit in a figure of eight; the Behemoth, black-scaled and bronze-horned, pacing, breathing fire, charging the glass when it sees you; Tiamat, living water that seeds its young up through the drains; the Eye of Judgment, which burns what it looks at for long enough; the Circle of Wings, which loves you and would like you closer; the Frog, with a hole where its head should be, which smiles; the Crystal Garden, growing out through its cracked glass into the hall (six touches and you're part of it); and the Hundred-Handed, behind bars. Bronze Wardens walk their rounds and fight whatever's got loose. ARIADNE's core is behind nine plates, and the board's machines are cutting through them. In Containment Control are the Heirs, Line H, grown from the board's own tissue to inherit the earth, and they make you an offer: a collar, or their teeth. Refuse, and they start opening the Behemoth's vault. Stop them, open the Tartarus Armory (the Prometheus Lance, the Stasis Projector, the Singularity Driver, a containment suit), kill the cutters before the ninth plate falls, and reconnect her.

**Five again, and the board.** ARIADNE has the building now, but she can't harm the board: she was made not to. She can open doors. The Praetorians and rail guns at the boardroom answer to the room, and the board's Enforcers come up behind you. Behind the shutters are five frightened people, and Voss making his case: there are courts for this; let the system decide; do anything else and you're what you think we are. Ask him what the Titans were for, and ARIADNE records the answer. Let them go, or don't. Seven endings, from *Good Pet* to *The Signal*: the best is the world hearing all of it, in his own voice.

**On every floor**, the HUD says what to do next (and what's optional), and the thing to find pulses when you're in its room and is marked on the minimap. Voss and ARIADNE stop the game when they speak, in the middle of the screen (Continue: E or Space; Skip: Esc), unless you're in a fight; the pause screen can put them back in the corner.

After the end come floors made in the editor on October 14, kept in the run as placeholders: *The Dead Signal Laboratory*, *The Complex* and *The Sterile Annex*.
