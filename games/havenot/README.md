# HaveNot: The Near-Area

A vertical slice of HaveNot (the JAGS setting) on the next-gen engine. Play it at `engine/index.html?game=havenot`, or pick it in the launcher.

You make a scavenger, leave a home bunker, and loot the near-area. A **mission** starts when you step outside and ends when you come back home: that's when kits and charges refill. Death ends the run.

## Levels and Threat Levels

After the books (Vol 1 p. 82, Vol 2 pp. 38–40): a character starts at Level 1. Every floor says its Threat Level (`hn.threat`; the HUD shows it beside yours, and arriving tells you whether it is your level or above you). Completing an installation of your level or higher (its `HN.INSTALLATIONS` flag: the agency's security closing, Princess Eggbot's death) levels you up: +15 max health, healed to full, and +1 to a stat of your choice. A lower one doesn't. An installation of Level N is stocked with Level N+1 gear (the weapon cards show the level). On a floor above your level enemy hits land 25% harder per level of difference (`HN.THREAT_GAP`), on top of monsters built for that level.

The balance runs (Playwright, the real engine driven by a scripted player of average aim) are summed up in `BALANCE.md`.

## Things to know when editing

- `hn-g-…` packs are made by the installation generator (`editor/gen/README.md`): The Bunker and the Installation-Ruins are generated in the game when a run starts (`hn-ruins`); `node tools/generate.mjs havenot --brief bunker --dry` previews a Bunker from the CLI. Its lists are in `gen/library/`, what's in the game in `gen/corpus.json`. Edit a generated pack like any other; the generator README says how to keep hand edits.

- Floor maps are generated by small Python scripts outside the repo, then hand-checked. Edit the level files directly from now on; the editor's map tools work on them.
- Story state lives in flags on the player (`hn…`) plus `LM.HN.st(game)` (saved with the run).
- Monsters never drop healing or ammo. Robots drop fuel cells and salvage; bio-weapons drop nothing.
- Between missions, Gladiator Roaches come back (up to 5 per floor), and while the Repair Bay runs it rebuilds up to 2 robots per floor. Nothing else respawns.
- `ART-PROMPTS.md` has the prompts for every picture and sprite that's still procedural.
