## Worth building next

The ten planned entries that would add the most variety, in order:

1. **looks / crypt**: Tombs currently borrow the bulkhead look; a stone-and-arches painter makes the tomb type look like itself.
2. **looks / toxic-waste**: Gives the toxin plant and the labs their own look: glowing barrels, sealed doors, a breathing hazard.
3. **puzzles / hangman-terminal**: A real logic puzzle for optional vaults (Vol 2 p. 140); data-driven, cheap to build.
4. **traps / flame-jets**: The first trial (Vol 2 p. 145): obvious, timed, with an off-switch on the far side.
5. **monsters / fungoids**: A sleeper-spawner bio-weapon for labs; adds a fight that changes on round two.
6. **monsters / dust-spiders**: A colony with a nest full of loot: a treasure set piece as well as a fight.
7. **locks / secret-door**: Optional rooms found by the Sense stat or a terminal (Vol 2 p. 68).
8. **monsters / cyberspine**: A proper sleeper boss for security floors (TL 4+).
9. **interest / teleport-console**: Shortcuts between consoles; makes large floors faster on the way back.
10. **monsters / hn-sphinx**: A generic riddle console would let the generator place Sphinx Guns with any slogan.
