# JAGS HAVENOT - 10 Settings Library (Batch 1)

Generated 10 settings x 20 rooms = 200 rooms

## Design Principles Identified
- Age of Wonders = impossible luxury, vanity, technology as magic, identity as product
- Age of War = bizarre militarization, resource scarcity, weapons with feelings
- Post-Collapse = machines faithfully doing terrible jobs, squatters misunderstanding ruins
- Humor = bureaucratic absurdity, narcissism, cruelty as customer service, tragic buried under garish
