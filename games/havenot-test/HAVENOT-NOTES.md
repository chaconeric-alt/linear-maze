# HaveNot (JAGS) -- Game-Design Digest

Sources: V1 = web_Vol_1_WORLD.txt, V2 = web_Vol_2_Adventure+.txt, V3 = web_Vol_3.txt.
Line numbers are approximate (~). All text paraphrased.

## 1. Tone and Look

HaveNot is post-post-apocalyptic science fantasy: a far future after two lost ages. The
**Age of Wonder** was a reality-bending, gamified paradise run on UbiNet (a computational layer
in subatomic reality) where social media became physical and life was "a game"; the **Age of
War** that followed was centuries of Great Warlords fighting over its leftovers with bio-weapons,
robots and terror-tech. "The Now" is scavengers, walled towns, raiders and cultists picking
through the bones. Ruins (V2 ~1338) are quiet, bleached of color by the sun, ash-dusted and
smelling burnt and metallic; light at dusk breaks into amber, sienna, jade and purple haze.
Cities are labyrinths of walls behind walls, and they also go *down*, merging into **The Complex**
far underground. Installations (the "dungeons") are dimly lit halls, laser barriers, old battle
stations with scrolling warning glyphs and soft chattering computers, sealed hatches with
military insignia, black/yellow hazard stripes, glowing "TRAP" lights over doors (Age of Wonder
doors honestly announce their own traps), smart doors that greet intruders, regenerating
warning glyphs, workplace-safety signage everywhere, insane motivational posters and vending
machines selling pills. The humor is dark and absurdist: machines still follow orders given
centuries ago, a coffee shop exists because an AI insisted it was "traditional".
Factions/inhabitants: Great Warlords' legacies (Tombs, Mech-Liches), raiders and brigands
(sometimes led by intelligent Armorbacks), Cultists (who favor Bolt Holes as bases), Evolved
Hyena tribes, robots and Artilects (from blunt guard bots to personality-enhanced ones),
Monoid slacker-robots, toxin monsters born from Age of Wonder "negative emotion" waste, and
avatars -- ghostly physical leftovers of social-media profiles still living out their routines.
Ruin types (V2 ~1440): Great Ruins, Sunken Installations, Age of War cities, Bolt Holes,
Civilian Structures (malls, offices), Tunnel Networks, Concrete Plains, Skyscraper Outcrops,
Decayed Compounds, and Tombs.

## 2. Monsters (V3 bestiary unless noted)

Danger = Threat Index (party level the stat block is tuned against); "win%" is simulated odds
of beating a 4-PC party. Monster type predicts loot: Toxin -> Pogs/Success Points/credits;
Vore -> swallowed weapons/armor; Robots -> power cells + sprockets (V3 ~3030).

1. **Gladiator Roach** (V3 ~3190) -- armored ceiling-dwelling bio-weapon roach in packs of 5-10.
   Drops from ceilings, nests in brown chitin balls; distractible by sweets. TI 1-2, swarm fodder.
2. **Knife Fighter** (V3 ~3345) -- small flying drone "balls of hate" with twin blades and an
   insult display. Fast and evasive but fragile; weak to melee and explosives. TI 1-3, groups of 4-8.
3. **Glorexx Tube Monster** (V3 ~3245) -- spring-tailed insectoid sleeping in wall stasis tubes;
   leaps out with claws and a taser bite every other round. TI 1-2, ambush trap-monster.
4. **Bombast Toad** (V3 ~3058) -- two-headed radioactive toad; glows brighter as it charges a
   plasma belch that explodes in a burning cloud. Lazy, won't chase far. TI 1-5. Kill it fast.
5. **Weaponized Maintenance Robot** (V3 ~3270) -- repair bot repurposed to kill, sometimes with a
   hateful personality. Slow (can't move and strike in one turn); confused by safety signs. TI 1-2.
6. **Monoid Robot** (V3 ~3310) -- lazy one-eyed robot that gambles and shirks; eye fires a power
   beam. Easily ambushed, may retreat from a hard fight, can be bribed (untrustworthy). TI 1-3.
7. **Fungoids** (V3 ~3135) -- fungal bio-weapon troops: Soldiers rush, claw-infect and explode
   (victims become Fungoids); Rippers have barbed tentacles; a Hive bursts spores. Slow; may lie
   dormant. Hive group = L3.
8. **Dust Spiders** (V3 ~3215) -- military-style colony (King, Queens, Soldiers); paralytic bite
   that wears off if you rest ~3 minutes; cocoon-filled nests hold victims' loot. TI 1-3.
9. **Stalking Worm** (V3 ~3525) -- vibration-sensing worms hiding in vents and lockers; drop or
   leap onto prey and burrow in. Slime trails and softball-sized eggs warn of them. TI 1-2, swarms.
10. **Wunk** (V3 ~3375) -- toxin-barrel spider with glowing pustules; phases through walls and
    armor, and a "single" Wunk can split into several superposed copies. TI 1-6, deceptively deadly.
11. **Heavy Security Drone** (V3 ~3290) -- 9-ft plodding guard robot with a scatter-blaster; sleeps
    in low-power surveillance mode. Hide in small spaces; it gives up and returns to its charger. TI 3-5.
12. **Zillixx Mk-1** (V3 ~3640) -- tentacled, toothy tube monster usually decanted in threes from
    a monster tank; raising an alarm releases many. TI 2-4.
13. **Cyberspine** (V3 ~3665) -- near-immortal installation guardian dormant in kennels; wakes on
    alarm/motion, opens doors, stalks, sees heat. Warning signs mention IFF chips. TI 4-7, elite.
14. **Leptosome** (V3 ~3420) -- stretchy tentacled toxin horror; grapples everything within 20
    yards with armor-ignoring phase tentacles; squeezes through soda-can holes; giggles while
    stalking; lured by candy. TI 4-7, mini-boss.
15. **Quockerwodger** (V3 ~3615) -- 9-ft orange glowing "Brain Bleach" toxin monster that
    ricochets off walls, hitting a primary target plus everyone nearby; glows when it spots you,
    deflates to hide in barrels. TI 5-8, boss.
16. **Ravener** (V3 ~3445) -- time-shifted pouncing hunters in 2-3s ("a haunt"); attack from behind;
    weak to explosives; lairs stocked with trophy gear. TI 6-9, deep-level threat.
17. **Sparkle-Vos Wasp** (V3 ~3822) -- glowing toxin wasp obsessed with credits; can be bought off
    (~100c each) and bursts into ~1000c when killed. TI 3-5, a walking treasure chest.
18. **Cyber Scorpion (Jaekelopterus)** (V3 ~3160) -- bio-cyber scorpions tracking radio signals;
    Mk-1 fight, Mk-2 hide, Mk-3 (TI 10) run.
Other usable names: Rave Raptors (party-drug pack hunters that can be danced/music-charmed),
War-Dogs (regenerating clever packs), Towering Blue-Claw (15-ft armored scavenger, bribe with
food), Armorback (intelligent bandit leader), Rail Guns / Sphinx Guns / auto-guns (V2 ~6300:
turrets; the Sphinx Gun asks riddle-like passcode questions and lasers wrong answers).

## 3. Treasure and Loot

Economy (V2 ~7008): treasure is budgeted per installation level in credits (L1 ~4000c, L3
~16000c, L5 ~44000c, L10 ~184000c for 4 PCs); ~20% is cash or sellables; resold gear fetches
only 10%. Types: weapons/armor (usually at the installation's level), Add-Ons, artifacts,
credits/sellables, expendables.

1. **Credits** -- physical UbiNet crypto-currency surviving from both ages; the base currency.
2. **Power Cells** -- scavenged from destroyed robots (small bots ~10c, bosses ~100c+); sold in towns.
3. **Sprockets** -- multi-function tech components from robots; crafting material for energy
   weapons with Engineering (V3 ~3045, V2 ~3628).
4. **Pogs (Karmic Tokens)** (V3 ~6560) -- poker-chip tokens with artwork, dropped by dying toxin
   monsters (usually 4 or 8 per drop, rolled on d10). Bind to a weapon, armor or person for a
   power; stacking same-type pogs strengthens it; removal destroys the pog. E.g., +2 armor,
   +2 melee damage and bonus health, or free blocks/dodges (V3 ~6540).
5. **Success Points** -- karmic "luck" points; also dropped by toxin monsters.
6. **Add-Ons / Power Fields** (V2 ~7180) -- weapon/armor enhancers worth 1-4 AP; Binding ones
   (e.g., stencils) fuse permanently; only one Power Field can be calibrated per person.
7. **Pill Packs** (V3 ~5715) -- Age of Wonder pills: Hard Body (bonus health + bronzed look),
   Embiggen (grow two sizes for 10 min), Placebo (chew ~24 for a full heal), Mystery Pack
   (random 2d6 effect: free skill, healing, delusion, hallucination).
8. **Combat Pills** (V3 ~5780) -- one-shot powers: Grenade Face (area blast), Power Burn (napalm
   jet), GhostPuncher (armor-ignoring phase punch), Full Autonomic (auto-fire from hands),
   Blockhead (instant block/dodge), "Antibiotic" (death ray), CyberFloss (robot body for 24 h).
9. **Smartphones** (V3 ~5860) -- near-indestructible relics; must be hacked to bond to you; run
   3 "apps" at a time that manipulate reality.
10. **Apps** (V3 ~5960) -- RockIT (hovering rocket you fire, 30-min recharge), Sledgehammer (ranged
    hammer aura), Niner-9 (summon a loaded pistol), Ringtone/Radiant (force fields), Telemetry
    (line-of-sight teleport 3/day), Medic! (daily healing), Exfil Plan (emergency party teleport to
    safety), Monstrosity (summons hostile tube monsters near you -- they attack anyone).
11. **Nabbit** (V3 ~6395) -- watch that becomes a blood-drinking mini-octopus, then bonds as a
    nagging advice-giving assistant/calendar. High value (~30000c).
12. **Vert Soda** (V3 ~6395) -- grenade-shaped soda; drinking lets you ask a cosmic consciousness
    one yes/no question (bad questions get "Mu").
13. **Vork-4 / Tellerms / Urums** (V3 ~6395) -- curio artifacts: shape-shifting armor-ignoring fork,
    tape spools that play alien music/lectures, linked tubes that keep flowing when separated.
14. **Vore gullet loot / Dust Spider cocoons / Dust Crawler holes / Ravener lairs** -- monster lairs
    as treasure rooms: dead adventurers' weapons, power armor, kit.
15. **Stasis bodies and Tomb hoards** (V2 ~1520) -- Tombs of Warlord officers hold their gear;
    some have fake treasure rooms as decoys.

## 4. Traps and Hazards (V2 ~4075-6660)

Damage scales with installation level (Low/Med/High/Lethal); detection is a Perception or Traps
skill roll vs. sensors; disarming costs "SP" in a skill drama. Age of Wonder doors often show a
lit TRAP light. Design rule: mix hidden low-damage traps with obvious lethal ones; only ~20%
should hit the whole party.

1. **Pit Traps** (~4415) -- pressure plate (100+ lb) opens a 10-12 ft pit. Variants: spiked,
   warning hiss (lets you jump clear), Nightingale (squeaky soft floor tiles; sneak past),
   Red Square (colored tiles), Drowning Pool, sealed oven pit, crush pit, slide, teleport drop,
   reverse-gravity ceiling pit, Garbage Disposal.
2. **Wide-Mouth Drop** (~4730) -- whole hallway floor splits open; triggered by opening one of
   many fake doors, then monsters arrive.
3. **Blade Traps** (~4753-4950) -- pneumatic broadsword-sized blade, ticking clockwork blade (audible
   warning gives a defense), torso-height horizontal guillotine, optical-beam spear piston,
   sound-activated ceiling drills, Harvester auto-scythe arm, Skewer Room (walls of blades),
   Death Fan of cleavers.
4. **Transparent Bear Traps** (~4766) -- near-invisible glass jaws; slow you until removed.
5. **Laser Beams** (~5045) -- invisible armor-ignoring cutting beams; a sweeping version gives a
   round of warning then scythes the hall for 4 rounds; a glowing node powers up before firing.
6. **Explosive Traps** (~5123-5270) -- micro-charge on a moved object/door, tripwire grenade,
   "I AM THE BOMB" remote charge that sings first, fragile collectible figurine bomb, rolling
   Bowling Bomb (dodgeable; its twin, the Party Bomb, just showers confetti), visible/hidden minefields.
7. **Gravity Well** (~5295) -- destroys a bit of space-time; everything within 4+ yards is dragged in (grapple).
8. **Sealing Room** (~5345) -- trigger locks all exits; room fills with water or sand over ~30
   rounds; quick reflexes can slip out at the moment of closing.
9. **Gun/Projectile Traps** (~5417-5530) -- single bullet, ninja star, hard ball, rocket (area),
   motion-triggered ceiling Damocles Laser (salvageable power cell), sound-activated
   flamethrower ("Wuff"), sensor-killing pulse, radioactive silhouette projector, fireball vents.
10. **Primogen Gas / toxins** (~5560) -- area chemical agent, hard to spot.
11. **Crushers** (~5713-5800) -- falling barrel, multi-ton weight, carved-face ceiling Blockhead that
    slams down on whoever passes under, corridor-wide rolling wheel (win initiative or be crushed).
12. **Electrical** (~5800-5935) -- electrified doorknob, live razor-wire, snake-striking live cables,
    lightning sphere dropped from the ceiling, electrified metal floor, sticky day-glo goop spray
    (bind + confuse), invisible soundproof force-field box.
13. **Monster Tube** (~6352) -- bio-weapon "trip-mine": sensor decants a sleeping monster;
    spotting it first gives the party a free attack round.
14. **Folgerizer** (~6385) -- teleports the victim into a holding cell and leaves a homicidal
    look-alike android in their place.
15. **Harassment** (~6400) -- e.g., blinding flash plus a kiosk selling your party photo; paint bomb.
Puzzle "Trials" (~6600-6660): a red-lit hall where a pursuing laser grid chases the nearest
person through zones (teamwork to open exits); "Logic Gates" rooms where you must choose
the door whose symbol matches your next room or set off a trap; floor flame-jet grid
(motion sensors with a hiss warning; sneak at a walk to cut down attacks).

## 5. Locations Usable as a Small Level

**Reified Semiotics (V2 ~2506-3640)** -- the books' running example, a Level-4 Age of
War research lab built by an Age of Wonder AI around a leaking "Negative Emotion Singularity".
Zones: *Employee* (Foyer, Information desk with a talking system, Main Lab, Break Room, Coffee
Shop) and *Security* (Checkpoint, back rooms behind security doors and a secret room).
Features: a 4D cubicle farm whose terminal shuffles which cubes exist in real space (sliding-block
puzzle; one cube hides an office-supply thief's stash); the BAD-BOSS 900 robot overseer; The Lab
with a rogue medical robot, logic-puzzle sealed lockers, a one-charge replicator tube that clones
a PC, and a stain on the floor that is a toxin monster; Smart Coffee that has turned into a bored,
aggressive toxin monster; a jammed red blast door in the break room; a washroom vending pills
and impossible objects; deranged motivational posters (and a trapped "emergency" gun cabinet).

**Bunker of the Atrocious Princess (V1 ~2865-2910)** -- small Age of War bunker for warlord's
daughter Anathema. Her reawakened social-media AI kidnaps locals with drones. Rooms: stairs down
several floors to a security chamber with two sleeping laser Observation Bots (block their
window by stealth); halls with leaking reactor radiation and a gate to pick inside the rad-zone;
sliding walls, razor-beam net, pressure-plate floors; security center; Systems room (shutdown
point); shrine to the Warlord with a skull pile (show respect or join it); medical room with the
blood-draining exsanguinator and maybe a medical bot; armory and vault at the back; a heavy guard
robot and flame units.

**The Great Cube / Tesseract Shops (V1 ~2920)** -- 70-ft featureless ceramic Age of Wonder cube
with a lit shop sign and one double door. Inside: six gravity planes (each face is a floor), a 3D
maze of teleporting doors, monsters and hostile "shopping" machines; you can't cross between
planes except through the doors.

**Robot Printer complex (V2 ~2040)** -- 20-room L3 site where a printer keeps manufacturing
robots: ~20 small bots, 4 mini-bosses, the Printer as boss (salvageable rocket gun, raw materials),
a dead adventurer with gear in room 5. Goal: find and disable the printer, loot, escape.

**Tombs (V2 ~1500)** -- warlord officers' self-defending crypts at Levels 5/10/15/20/30: stasis
bodies at the bottom, fake treasure rooms, Mech-Liches in cyber-sarcophagi, monument tombs with
trapped display cases.
