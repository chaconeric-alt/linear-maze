# HaveNot: art prompts

Prompts for the pictures and sprites the slice still draws procedurally. Every one of them has working procedural art in the game today, so these can be made in any order and swapped in one at a time.

**House style for everything below.** Retro-future wasteland, three hundred years after the Age of War. Bright, sun-bleached desert colour outside (sand, rust, faded turquoise, signal orange); inside the agency, clinical white, pink neon and corporate pastel gone grimy. Clean shapes, readable silhouettes, a little absurd. Humour comes from the objects themselves (a cheerful logo on something lethal), never from cartoon faces winking at the camera. No text in images unless a prompt quotes it exactly.

## 1. Image collateral

For an image generator. Size noted per group. Map pins are shown in the location panel at 480 × 270 (16:9).

### Map pins (16:9, painted, wide establishing shot, late afternoon light)

- **Home.** A small sealed bunker hatch set into a low sandy rise, one blinking antenna, a hand-painted welcome mat in front of a blast door, scrub brush, long shadows. Lonely but safe.
- **Out-rider Outpost.** Part bunker, part mobile home, ringed by four mismatched radar dishes and antennas on a dusty plateau. A patched generator shed, a jeep with no wheels on blocks, laundry on a line, a hand-lettered sign "OUTPOST: WE BUY CELLS".
- **Boom-Town.** A walled corporate town of adobe and corrugated metal behind a gate with a robot gatekeeper in a booth. Billboards with smiling mascots over the wall: "OPPORTUNITY IS MANDATORY". A dome-roofed clinic, a bazaar under awnings, an arms dealer's sign "BANG & SONS".
- **Mother Long-Legs.** A dark hole in a rocky outcrop, edges hung with silk that catches the light like rain. A few glowing blue mushrooms at the mouth. Eight faint pinpoints of light deep inside. Not frightening, ancient and gentle.
- **The Anomalous Structure.** A square three-storey building in distressed concrete standing alone in the desert, too intact. Mirrored windows, a cracked rooftop billboard still lit in pink: "WANT MORE. WORK MORE. BE MORE." A dead parking lot, a revolving door.
- **The Bunker (Installation B).** A heavy concrete pillbox half-buried in a dune, blast door sealed with a wheel lock, stencilled numbers, sand drifts against it.
- **The Warren (Installation C).** A field of ventilation shafts and collapsed skylights poking out of the ground, as if something huge is buried and breathing.
- **The Bolt Hole.** A rusty hatch under a dead billboard west of the Outpost; the billboard shows a faded smiling family with a TV. Footprints in the sand lead to it and stop.
- **The Greasy Bolt Hole.** A rusted, grease-blackened hatch standing open in a fresh scoop between two dunes, wind still moving sand off its lid. Stencilled in dried grease: "BOLT HOLE 9 · TRESPASSERS WILL BE GREASED". A ladder going down into darkness, an oily rainbow sheen on the sand around it, and something small and segmented glinting on the rim.
- **The Mushroom (locked).** On the horizon: a grey dome on a stalk, taller than anything for a day's walk, haze around its base.
- **The Freeway (locked).** A shattered elevated roadway on concrete legs, running off into the distance, sections missing.
- **The Tall Stacks (locked).** Smokestacks on the horizon still breathing a little smoke on a cold morning, pale sky.

## 2. Character sprites

For a sprite tool such as Sprite cook. Format: the engine's drawn sprite sheets (SPEC §22.4, `editor/templates/SPRITE-SHEETS.md`). **Three-quarter view, 4 directions (east, south, west, north), 64 × 80 frames**, template `editor/templates/sheet-34-4dir-64x80.png`. Rows in order idle (4), walk (6), attack (6, hit on frame 4), hurt (2), death (6), each with a row per direction. Feet on the pink cross, top-left soft light, 1–2 px dark outline, no painted shadow or glow, transparent PNG.

- **Human scavenger.** A wiry wastelander in a patched duster and goggles pushed up on the forehead, a satchel across the body, scarf over the lower face. Variants: holding the Frontier Handgun; the Pump Scattergun in both hands with a heavy chest plate; the Gauss Pistol with no armour. Attack row: the gun recoils on frame 4.
- **Mutant Hyena.** An upright hyena-man, lean and fast, spotted fur, long jaw, ragged vest, ears back. Attack row: a lunging bite on frame 4. Walk is a loping half-crouch.
- **Random Mutant.** One base body (human proportions, grey-green skin) plus overlay variants that match the mutations: chitin plates on the shoulders, a third arm, stalk eyes, a bulging cranium, glowing skin (paint it pale cyan, no glow), quills down the back, an acid-spitting jaw (green drool). Make each variant a separate sheet on the same body so they line up.

## 4. Monsters and robots

Base each on the Vol 3 illustrations. Sprite-tool format as in section 2; size and view noted per monster. Robots are agency white and pink with black-and-yellow hazard stripes; bio-weapons are glossy and wrong-coloured.

- **Weaponized Maintenance Robot.** Three-quarter, 4 dir, 64 × 80. A squat tracked robot with a mop arm and a welding arm, cheerful agency logo on its chest, a speaker grille that shouts insults ("UR MOM IS A TOASTER"). Attack: the welding arm jabs on frame 4. Death: it tips over, sparking.
- **Knife Fighter.** Three-quarter, 4 dir, 64 × 80. A thin bipedal security robot, all elbows, four knife hands, a smiley face screen. Fast. Attack: a flurry, blow lands on frame 4. Death: collapses into a pile of knives.
- **Monoid.** Three-quarter, 4 dir, 64 × 80. A one-eyed slouching robot in a loosened tie, "on break since the Age of War", a credit chip in its hand. Idle: feeding a slot machine (can be the same pose looped). Fighting: the eye turns red, it stands straight. Death: the eye goes dark.
- **Sphinx Gun.** Top-down, 128 × 128, one direction (the game turns it). A squat turret on a reception desk with a stylised sphinx face plate, a laser where the nose should be. Attack: the nose glows (frames 1–3) and fires on frame 4. Death: face plate cracks and slides off.
- **Heavy Security Drone.** Top-down, 128 × 128. A wide hovering disc with four rotors, a searchlight eye and an underslung gun, agency pink trim. Idle: rotors blur. Death: rotors stop, it drops and bursts.
- **Gladiator Roach.** Top-down, 64 × 64. A dog-sized cockroach, glossy amber-brown, with a gladiator's helmet grown into its head and spiked forelegs. Walk: skittering legs. Attack: forelegs snap on frame 4. Death: flips over, legs curl.
- **Glorexx (stasis tube).** Three-quarter, 1 dir, 128 × 160. A bio-weapon in a cracked glass tube: a mass of lavender flesh, many small mouths, tentacles pressed against the glass, labelled "SPECIMEN: GLOREXX · DO NOT TAP". Second sheet for after release: the creature out of the tube, three-quarter, 4 dir, 128 × 160, oozing forward on its tentacles.
- **Desktop Defender.** Top-down, 64 × 64. A palm-sized furious robot that comes out of a box marked DESK SECURITY: tiny tank treads, two stapler jaws, a single angry LED. Attack: staples fire on frame 4. Death: pops a spring and goes still.
