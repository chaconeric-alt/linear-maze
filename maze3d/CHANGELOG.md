# maze3d: changelog

What changed and when, newest first. The reference for how things are now is `SPEC.md`; how to work on the code is `CLAUDE.md`.

## 2026-11-22

- **Programme levels of two floors** (the generator's third step; SPEC 6.3). A programme's `below` is a second programme laid out on the storey under it, in the same level (storey -1: the level's `floors` entry, its things at `"level": -1`), from the level's seed + 1. `ProgrammeFloors` (new, `generators/programme/floors.gd`) lines the two up by their lift bank (the room both name in `bank`, the same size on both), so the bank's lifts (`lifts`: `count`, `travel`, `start`) stand in the same cells on both floors, along a wall with no door, and stop on both. An upper room with `"down": 1` is a hall sunk a storey: carved out of the floor below, its upper doors opening on to galleries with two-cell ramps down to its floor; a lower room with `"height": 2` rises into the floor above, with a door on to a gallery where a corridor runs beside it. A ramp needs plain floor past its foot (two ramps foot to foot in a narrow hall cut its floor off: the reach part found it), and nothing the fill or the furnishing puts in stands on a ramp or a lift's cell. `ProgrammeCheck` now walks storeys: each floor's map, decks at their height, ramps, lifts. `Schema` checks `below`, `bank`, `down`, `lifts`. Also fixed: furniture that the level hangs on a wall itself (`"mount": "wall"`: lockers, wall terminals) was given the furnishing's offset as well and stood in the rock; it now gets none (the Blueprint's maps and rooms are as they were at seeds 0 and 7, its lockers and terminals now against the wall). The Blueprint, Two Floors (`blueprint2`, `tools/levels/blueprint2.py`, its own test), in the menu and the baseline. Baseline item32: 25 runs, 3607 checks, 0 failures; against item30 every line is there but the title menu's count (25 to 26 buttons; the save part's count moves by one in three runs with it), the bunker lift's (10 to 11 places), and the Blueprint's, changed by the second step's deeper dormitory and its furniture; every other digest the same.

- **Programme rooms furnished for their purpose** (the generator's second step). A programme room's `purpose` names one of the game's purposes (new `defs/purposes.json`, 31 for a military bunker: bunk rooms, latrines, the mess hall, offices, the armoury, wards, the morgue, an archive...), and the `rooms` generator's own furnishing puts it in (`ProgrammeFurnishing`): four seeds a room, the fullest kept, so small rooms still get their tables and beds; the `rooms` generator is unchanged. `Schema` checks the purposes; the programme's `furnish` scales the amount. The Blueprint's rooms have purposes (its dormitory one cell deeper, for its bunks) and its test checks every room of twelve cells or more is furnished from its purpose and nothing stands in a doorway's way; it passes in four worlds (seeds 0, 7, 18, 20).

- **Levels laid out from a written programme** (the last of the shared gaps, first step; SPEC 6.3). A level file can hold a `programme` in place of a map: spine rooms in order from the start to the end, wings that branch off the spine with their rooms on both sides of a branch corridor (`next_to`, `door_to`), locks and their cards, each room's things. `ProgrammeGen` (`generators/programme.gd` and `generators/programme/`: `ProgrammePlan`, `ProgrammeLayout`, `ProgrammeFill`, `ProgrammeCheck`) lays it out when it is entered, from the world's seed (`State.world_seed()`, new, drawn the first time a world needs one) or `--seed`; `--dump` writes the result. The seed picks what Eric left to it: sizes within ranges, wing sides and where a wing branches, which side each room goes, optional rooms (`chance`), the card's room, a thing's kind from a list, the gaps. The proof (`ProgrammeCheck.solve`) walks the map with the cards found so far; a seed that fails is tried again as seed + 7919. `Schema` checks programme keys. The Blueprint (`blueprint`, `tools/levels/blueprint.py`, its own test: the rules, thirty worlds, a programme written wrong, then the standard parts played on it). In the menu and the baseline. Baseline item30: 24 runs, 3534 checks, 0 failures; item29's lines all there but the title menu's count (24 to 25 buttons; its one more frame moves the save part's count in four runs) and the bunker lift's (9 to 10 places); every digest the same.

- **Making a character at the bunker** (`HomeCreation`, `engine/home/creation.gd`): the 2D game's creation screen as a panel at the ledger. A new scavenger (a new world's, or the next after retiring) is not made: arriving at the bunker says the ledger asks who they are, and the ledger offers it until they are. A name from the game's list, an origin (a Random Mutant's mutations rolled from a seed and rolled again once), three skill points, and "This is me": the record, the player and the save. The ledger now shows the character (name, origin, stats, skills). Words in `defs/home.json` (`make_*`, `ledger_character`, `ledger_make`, `made_say`); `CharacterSheet.roll_mutations`, `origin_name`. The bunker's test makes a Mutant Hyena with Hacking 2 and Medicine 1 and finds them so after the reload (six new checks; the home, Descent and story runs otherwise line for line the same).

- **The character: stats and skills** (the fourth of the shared gaps; Eric: the 2D game's stats and the three skills recommended, built so that more can be added). `CharacterSheet` (`engine/character_sheet.gd`) works out what a character's choices come to from the game's new `defs/character.json` (the four stats, the three skills, the three origins and the mutations, from the 2D game's `hn-rules/character.js`) and `character` rules: stats within 1 to 7, and what each stat, skill rank and mutation gives by name (`hp`, `move`, `pack`, `slots`, `hack`, `heal`, `sense`, `armor`, `lamp`; `Schema` checks them). A character's record keeps its `origin`, `mutations`, `skills` and `made` (save version 5: one from before is a Human with nothing, made). Where it acts: health from Physical, speed, natural armour and the lamp (`Player.apply_character`), healing (`Player.heal`, Medicine; a health pickup says what it came to), hands and pack (`Carrying`), hack odds (`Game.hack_chance`: Mental and Hacking), the new panel gate `skill` (`{"mechanics": 2}`, label `[Mechanics 2]`), and the map, where Sense shows unseen monsters and treasure within (4 + Sense) cells through walls (`HudMap.sensed`). A saved player's health comes back as saved (`SavePoint.put_back`) rather than through the settings, where Physical would count twice. The 2D mutations that need a player's melee are left out (Quills, Acid Spit); the Third Arm holds a third weapon. New self-test part `character` (after `state`, on every level, waiting no frames); the `state` part checks the old file's character becomes a Human, made. Baseline item29: 23 runs, 3454 checks, 0 failures; every item28 check still passes, the same in every line but the state check's version (4 to 5), and every events and keys digest the same.

## 2026-11-21

- **Explosives as a damage class, and a rocket launcher** (the third of the shared gaps). The game's `damage_types` list the kinds of hurt (HaveNot: physical, with `bullet`, `melee`, `crush`, `trap` and `fall` as its aliases; explosive, alias `blast`; poison, alias `toxic`; beam, alias `laser`; fire, energy, acid, shock); `DamageTypes` (`engine/damage_types.gd`) reads a body's `takes` by the kind's own name first, else by any name of the same type, so every `takes` written before means what it did. A projectile's `blast` now says its `kind`, `knock` and `lift` (thrown back and up, less with distance, by the body's mass), `self` (the player's own blast hurts them: `self` was read before, but the player, as the shot's owner, was always passed over) and `fireball` (`Fx.fireball`: a white-hot core in a swelling ball of fire, and a ring of smoke on the floor; its own materials, tweens only). A dash outruns every hurt but an explosive one, by type. A hit's own `knock` now throws an actor as it did the player (so in faction fights an attack's knock counts; the Yard's two fights take 2.3 and 4.3 s instead of 2.5 and 3.4, passing at seeds 7, 18 and 20). The Rocket Launcher (`launcher`, `rockets`, at most 6; a crate of two; the rocket: 120 on a direct hit, 90 in 4.5 m, knock 11, lift 4, hurts its user) with models of its own (`weapon_launcher`, `item_ammo_rockets`, `tools/blender/props.py`) and a sound; on the Staff Only test floor. The actors part leaves a weapon that hurts its user out of its rotation (its fights are those of before). The Proving Range (`range`, `tools/levels/range.py`): a lane of custodian dummies and a back room behind a wall, with its own test (aliases, the old `takes`, falloff, knock and lift, no blast through a wall, the launcher picked up and fired, the back blast); in the menu and the baseline. Baseline item28: 23 runs, 3224 checks, 0 failures; every check of item27 still passes. What changed in them: the title menu has 24 buttons (and the menu part waits a frame a button, so the save part in the Crypt, gen:rooms 22, the Hospital and the Megaplex finds one thing more or less remembered); the bunker's lift has nine places; the Descent's record of the player lists `rockets`; Staff Only has two more pickups (two more taken, 25 cr more treasure) and its later fights shift.

The baseline is unchanged check for check and digest for digest; the Basilica passes at 20 local seeds, the deep and crypt themes at three. SPEC 5.14.
- **Factions and patrols** (the second of the shared gaps). The game's `factions` say which sides are at war (HaveNot: `robots` and `monsters`, each at war with the player only, so every existing level plays exactly as before); a level's own `factions` replace the game's entry for every side they name; war goes both ways (`Factions`, `engine/actor/factions.gd`). Every actor def has a `faction`; an entry's `faction` overrides it, and `player` makes an ally. New `ActorFoes` (`engine/actor/foes.gd`), added only where a side is at war with another, or not with the player, or the level places allies: four times a second the nearest enemy it can see (checking the four nearest), kept while seen unless something 40% nearer turns up, else what it had, else the player if at war with them; it turns on an enemy that hurts it, not on its own side; a neutral hurt by the player goes after the player; an ally never turns. `ActorCore.target()` asks it. A monster's ranged shot at another monster can hit monsters (`Projectile.at_actors`); a grapple strikes a monster it cannot hold; an attack left half done is dropped when there is nothing to fight. New `ActorPatrol` (`engine/actor/patrol.gd`): an entry's `patrol` (cells in order, a tag, or `{route, pause, give_up}`) is walked at a walk, pausing at each point; after `give_up` seconds without seeing what it was after, back to the beat from the nearest point. With nothing to fight an actor with factions or a beat goes about its business instead of standing still. Dying moved from `ActorCore._die` into `ActorRemains.die` (and the dead tick into `ActorRemains.dead_tick`, waking the others nearby into `ActorSenses.wake_others`): `actor_core.gd` is back under budget (385 lines). Only the player's own sight puts a monster on the map. New test level the Yard (`yard`, `tools/levels/yard.py`), in the menu and the baseline, with its own test. Schema: actor def `faction`; actor entry `faction`, `patrol`. SPEC 3.1, 3.3, 4, 4.5, 5.5, new 5.20, 7, 9.

New self-test part `state` on every level: the version-0 fixture (`tests/fixtures/havenot_home_v0.json`) migrates, a world round-trips, retiring keeps the bunker, the fronts read `State`, all in memory. Saving to and reading from disk, `.tmp` and `.bak` checked by hand.
- **Saved state, step 2: the event bus.** New autoload `Events` (`engine/events.gd`): `emit`, `on`, `off`; payloads must be plain data (anything else is reported and counted); listeners whose owner is gone are dropped. Emitted: `level.entered`, `level.left`, `run.started`, `run.ended` (`Main`, which now keeps the level's key: `gen:...#<seed>` for a generated one), `actor.died` (`ActorRemains.leave`), `item.taken` (`Pickup`), `thing.used` (`Prop.use`, `Interaction.choose`), `door.changed` (`Door`, lock and seal changes only), `flag.set` and `credits.changed` (`Game`). Things made while playing outside `spawn_thing` now get keys too: loot is its container's key and `/loot.n`, a monster's drops, a `drop` effect's items and bunker furniture `extra:n` (`Spawner.keep`). New self-test part `events`, last on every level: the run's events held up against the level (entry, deaths, takings, flags, plain payloads, event triggers), with counts and a digest.
- **Saved state, step 1: stable keys.** Every thing in a level now has a key from where it is in the level's data, the same on every load: `door:x,y,f`, `obj:x,y`, `thing:n` (`thing:n@x,y` for a tag), parts with a suffix (`/robot`, `/seat`, `/holds.0`), things made while playing `extra:n`. `Spawner.build_things` walks `Spawner.plan()`, the data's steps with their keys (one walk, so the build and the keys cannot disagree); `Level.things`, `Level.key_of(node)`, `Level.thing(key)`. `Interaction` keeps used marks by key instead of the node's id, which is what will let them be saved. New self-test part `keys`, run first on every level (and before a level's own test): every key comes from the data, a second read of the data gives the same keys, the marks use them; it prints a digest of the keys. Nothing in play changes.
- **Traps hurt monsters and robots.** Every trap used to hurt the player only; now it hurts whatever is in it, unless its new `ignore` spares `"robots"`, `"monsters"` (living things) or the `"player"` (a word or a list). Damage goes through `takes` as before, by the trap's type (`laser`, `shock`, `toxic`, `trap`). Walkers do not see traps coming: in the baseline, more monsters now die in corridor traps chasing the player before the test gets to fight them.

Before moving they look ahead along the way, every half metre, and ask `Level.hazard()` (each `Trap.danger()`) whether their body would be hurt there now or before they could be through, a time worked out from their size and speed. ## 2026-11-20: the maintainability refactor (branch `maze3d-refactor`)

A structural cleanup. The game plays as it did; formats, def keys, level files and kit files are unchanged except where noted.

1. **Effects.** One `EffectRegistry` (`engine/effects.gd`): effect type to handler. `Game`, `Level` and `Prop` use types and the HUD's option keys register into it (`engine/level/level_effects.gd`, `engine/prop_uses.gd`, `engine/option_gates.gd`). `Game.run_effects` kept its signature.
2. **Interaction.** Everything the HUD's `_choose` did except drawing moved to `engine/interaction.gd` (`Game.interaction`): conditions, costs, hack and risk rolls, "used" marks (kept by the thing's id, not written into level dictionaries), effects, callbacks. Command-line test switches became `engine/debug.gd` (`Debug`), set by `Main` and by the self-test.
3. **Level split.** `engine/level.gd` became a thin node that owns `LevelData`, `LevelBuilder` (with `LevelCollision`, `LevelNav`, `LevelFluid`, `LevelEnvironment`), `LevelLights`, `Spawner` (a registry of factories by entry kind), `LevelSigns`, `Triggers` and the F1–F3 `LightingDebug`, all in `engine/level/`. Its public methods are unchanged.
4. **Actor split.** `engine/enemy.gd` (`Enemy`) became `engine/actor/`: `ActorCore` and `Actor`, with component nodes (`ActorSenses`, `ActorLocomotion`, `ActorClimb`, `ActorAttacks`, `ActorAimRig`, `ActorLooks`, `ActorLounge`, `ActorOffDuty`, `ActorConversation`) added only when their def key is present, an explicit state (dormant, idle, alert, attacking, stunned, climbing, off duty, dead) and one `target()`.
5. **Player split.** `engine/player.gd` keeps the body, health and use; `engine/player/` has `PlayerControls`, `PlayerMovement`, `PlayerCamera` and `PlayerWeapons`.
6. **Generator pipeline.** `generators/rooms.gd` `_try` (one 1,100-line function) became named stages over a `GenContext` in `generators/rooms/`. Same seed, same level, byte for byte.
7. **Content out of code.** The bunker's catalogue, prices, repair recipe, robot, broadcasts and programmes are `games/havenot/defs/home.json`; the generator's locker table is `generator_params.lockers` in `game.json`. (New data; nothing old changed.)
8. **Schema.** `engine/schema.gd` checks the keys of actors, items, weapons and level things against the lists in `SPEC.md`, at boot and in the self-test. `SPEC.md` gained the keys the engine read without documenting them (`gib`, `still` and `turn` on actors, `kit`, `text` and `trophy` on items, `greet` on things).
9. **Tools.** `tools/levels/levelkit.py` holds what the level scripts shared. The Blender kit scripts are named for their kits (`kits3.py` to `kits8.py` became `kits_works_bunker_crypt.py`, `kits_executive_megaplex_hospital_basilica.py`, `kits_robots_loot.py`, `kits_drowned_dust.py`, `kits_home.py`, `kits_anomalous.py`).
10. **Tests.** `engine/selftest.gd` became `tests/` (a runner and one part per feature); the bunker's and the Anomalous Structure's own tests sit beside their levels (`games/havenot/levels/<id>_test.gd`). Tests use public methods only.
11. **Documentation.** `SPEC.md` is one section per subsystem; this file holds the history; `CLAUDE.md` is new.
